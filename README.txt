HOW TO ADD PHOTOS AND CAPTIONS:
1) Put your 14 photos inside the images folder.
2) Name them exactly photo01.JPG through photo14.JPG. Capital JPG matters on GitHub.
3) Under each photo in index.html, change the text between <figcaption> and </figcaption>.
4) Pressing “Open Your Gift” automatically starts the song from 1:35. The Play button is kept as a backup/control.
5) Upload/replace index.html, script.js, style.css, and the images folder in your GitHub repository.
